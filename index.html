Our Story

We Started Because
We Were Frustrated.

In 2021, we watched a brilliant local business — great product, passionate founder, loyal customers — completely invisible online. Not because they lacked effort. Because they lacked the right team.

That frustration became our purpose. We built NOVA to give every brand, big or small, the same unfair advantage that the big players have: a dedicated creative team that treats your business like their own.

We don't just deliver files. We show up, we strategise, we obsess over details — and we celebrate every win with you.
